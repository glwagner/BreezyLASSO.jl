# Results

Figures and lightweight summaries of the committed production runs. Each run directory in
`output/` (not versioned) carries a `provenance.toml`; the record is copied here next to the
figures as `<run>_provenance.toml`. `summary.md` in each subdirectory is regenerated by
`analysis/plot_results.jl` (`RESULTS_SUBDIR=<name> julia --project=analysis analysis/plot_results.jl output/<run> ...`).

Every run uses the public Covert et al. (2022) SAM inputs and is a **Covert-public-bin
development benchmark**, not an official LASSO-ENA reproduction (that needs the restricted
`samin` archive). All runs: 256 × 256 columns at 35 m over 8.96 km, Float32, fixed Δt = 0.5 s
(the namelist `dt`), 6 h from 06 UTC 18 July 2017, one A100 each, prescribed surface fluxes,
`rad_simple` longwave, Smagorinsky–Lilly, WENO(5) with bounds-preserving limiters on every
water mass and positivity-only limiters on the number/volume moments (`moment_advection =
positive`, the default since b584225).

## Reading the two trios

- Both grids give the same picture: the 1M control holds LWP at 120–135 g m⁻² with weak drizzle
  (0.1–0.5 mm day⁻¹), while both P3 members thicken the cloud to 200–240 g m⁻² over the six
  hours with in-cloud w² of 0.5–0.6 m² s⁻² versus 0.3 for 1M. The prognostic-aerosol member has
  nearly the same LWP as N75 but its drizzle is suppressed by two orders of magnitude
  (0.001 versus 0.2–0.3 mm day⁻¹), the expected aerosol–drizzle signature.
- Grid sensitivity (10 m versus 25 m vertical spacing) is modest for all three schemes:
  ±5 g m⁻² of LWP for 1M and ≈ 30 g m⁻² for P3.
- Pins: the two 1M runs were made on Oceananigans `877618e`; the four P3 members on `c78eeaa`
  (the limiter 0/0 fix), all with Breeze `5fc404c`, recorded in each provenance file.

## Superseded runs (not committed, documented in the README)

Covert-grid P3 members on plain-moment advection (jobs 860, 862) died within 21 min of the
rain-number runaway; the first positivity-limited Covert-grid members (874, 875) and the first
LASSO-grid members (872, 873) ran on Oceananigans `877618e` and died from the limiter's 0/0
corner at 100 s, 2.1 h, 5.7 h and (cancelled at 2.5 h) respectively before the fix in `c78eeaa`.
